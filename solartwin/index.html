Executive Recommendation

The proposed AI-first engineering model is directionally correct but incomplete. Solar Twin should adopt AI as a productivity accelerator, translator and auditor while retaining human ownership of architecture, compliance, security and strategic decisions. Governance, technical sovereignty and accountability must be established before scaling AI adoption.

Independent Assessment

The proposal focuses on productivity but underestimates Security, Ownership, Liability, Vendor Lock-In and Technical Debt. The key challenge is maintaining technical ownership, architectural quality and operational security while increasing AI usage and relying on external development teams.

  • Security-by-Design
  • IP Ownership & Vendor Management
  • Liability & Accountability
  • Technical Debt Prevention
  • Architectural Sovereignty
